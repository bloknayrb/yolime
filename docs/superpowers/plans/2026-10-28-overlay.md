# PR4 `feat/overlay` Implementation Plan (v1, for multi-angle review)

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** the notch. It shows what yolime is doing without ever taking focus:
- the states Listening, Working, Done and Error;
- the preview card, an undo, and the pending paste;
- the sounds and Narrator announcements;
- high contrast, DPI and multi-monitor support.

**Spec:** `docs/plan.md`, sections Overlay, Output (Preview, Undo, Pending paste), Capture ("No audio: is the mic muted?") and No lost work ("Retry from the overlay error card").

**Rendering decision (task 0, proven before this plan was reviewed):**
- A raw Win32 popup hosts a `DesktopWindowXamlSource` island. The popup is `WS_POPUP`, with the extended styles `WS_EX_NOACTIVATE | TOPMOST | TOOLWINDOW`, plus `TRANSPARENT | LAYERED` while it is click-through, and `WM_MOUSEACTIVATE → MA_NOACTIVATE`.
- The spike's WinUI `Window` shown without activation sometimes never drew. The prototype has to pass a Sandbox scenario three runs in a row: shown, actually painted (pixel check) and the target still in the foreground. The results go in the PR body.

## Decisions

1. **The model is pure Core; the view is thin App.**
   - `Core/Overlay/OverlayModel` is a reducer: `(OverlayView, OverlayInput) → OverlayView`. It holds no timers; time comes in as `Tick(nowMs)` inputs.
   - `OverlayView` is a record: the state (Hidden, Listening, Working, Done, Error, Preview, PendingPaste), the title, the detail, the preview text, the elapsed seconds, the level, the buttons, a clickable flag, and an optional narration.
   - The App renders a view into the island, and that is all it does. All rules can be tested.
2. **Inputs** come from what already exists, plus a few new events:
   - capture start and commit (coordinator), mode cycle, level (`CaptureLevel`), and the context snippet (coordinator, after the read);
   - `StageStarted`/`TranscriptReady`/`RewriteDelta` (pipeline progress, now passed by the coordinator);
   - the `RunResult` and the notices;
   - `Tick`.

   The coordinator gets an `IProgress<PipelineEvent>` for the run, plus a new `OverlayInput` event stream. The hook thread never touches XAML; everything is marshalled to the UI dispatcher.
3. **What each state shows** (spec text):
   - **Listening:** mode label (Dictate, Prompt, Reply, Raw), the app's display name, elapsed m:ss, a level meter. For Reply and Prompt, the context line is "Replying to: '…' (selection | clipboard, N s old)", or "No context" when the read returned nothing.
   - **Silence:** after 2 s with no voiced chunk, Listening's detail becomes "No audio: is the mic muted?". This is the same RMS threshold as the speech gate.
   - **Working:** "Transcribing…", then "Writing for Slack…", with elapsed seconds. The detail is the raw transcript, then the streamed output (last 2 lines, trimmed).
   - **Done:** "→ Slack · 42 words · 1.9 s" for 1.2 s. The times come from `RunTiming.TotalMs` and `OutputWords`. When delivery was Copied, it says "Copied · press Ctrl+V".
   - **Error:** the notice text (enum-driven, no content) and Retry when the run is retryable. It stays 8 s or until clicked or dismissed (the × button), and is **clickable**.
   - **Run notices move from tray balloons to the overlay.** Balloons stay for things that happen with the overlay hidden: Started, AlreadyRunning, and startup key problems.
4. **Click-through is the default.** Error, Preview and Undo turn click-through off while they show, and back on when they hide.
5. **Preview card**:
   - Triggers: Shift held at release (`RunIntent.PreviewRequested`, already plumbed), or the per-mode setting `PreviewModes` (default: none), or output over `PreviewLongOverWords` words (default 0 = off).
   - It shows the whole output, scrollable, with "Enter insert · Esc discard · C copy".
   - **The keys are captured by the keyboard hook**: a new `HookReducer` capture mode swallows Enter, Esc and C (no modifiers) while the preview is up. Focus never moves.
   - `IOutputSink` gains a preview step through a port `IPreviewPrompt.AskAsync(text, ct) → Insert | Discard | Copy`. The executor calls it after planning and before the clipboard swap.
   - Discard keeps the history row (outcome Discarded, already in the pipeline). Preview times out after 60 s: copy, with a notice.
6. **Undo ("Use raw instead")**:
   - For 5 s after a Dictate, Prompt or Reply paste that was rewritten, Done shows a clickable "Use raw" button.
   - **Clicking it, in a non-terminal target whose foreground and focus are unchanged:** send Ctrl+Z once, then paste the raw transcript, through the same executor path.
   - **In terminals, or when the target changed:** copy the raw transcript, with a notice. Ctrl+Z suspends a shell job.
   - It is never offered for Raw, for fallbacks or for copied deliveries.
7. **Pending paste**:
   - When the planner returns `CopiedNotEditable` for **Reply**, the executor asks a new port, `IEditableFocusWaiter.WaitAsync(10 s, ct)`, instead of copying at once.
   - The App implements it with a WinEvent focus hook plus the existing UIA editable check, which takes the first editable focus in any window.
   - The overlay shows "Click where the reply should go" with a countdown. A timeout or a cancel copies, with the notice as today.
   - The text sits on our transient clipboard only during the paste. While waiting, it is held in memory, never on the clipboard.
8. **Sounds**:
   - The existing `MessageBeep` sounds become short embedded WAVs played through `PlaySound(SND_ASYNC|SND_MEMORY)`: start, stop, error, and mode cues of 1–4 soft ticks.
   - They're generated at build time by a small script, so no binary assets come from elsewhere. `Settings.Sounds` gates them all, as now.
9. **Narrator**:
   - On each state change with narration (Listening start, Done, Error, Preview shown), the island's root raises `AutomationPeer.RaiseNotificationEvent(ImportantMostRecent)` with enum-built text.
   - Transcripts are never announced, except the preview card, which is user-invoked.
10. **Appearance**:
    - Theme resources follow light and dark (`ActualTheme` from the system).
    - High contrast uses the `SystemColor*` brushes.
    - Text scaling follows `UISettings.TextScaleFactor`, and the notch height grows with it.
    - `UISettings.AnimationsEnabled` gates the fade.
    - DPI comes from the target monitor, recomputed per show. Moving between monitors is covered by `ShowAt` reading the foreground's monitor each time.
11. **Position:** `OverlayPosition` = TopCentre (default), BottomCentre or NearCaret.
    - NearCaret uses `GetGUIThreadInfo.rcCaret` mapped to screen coordinates, falling back to TopCentre.
    - The position is chosen at show time and doesn't move during a run.
12. **Screen sharing:** a `HideOverlayFromCapture` setting (default off) applies `SetWindowDisplayAffinity(WDA_EXCLUDEFROMCAPTURE)`.
13. **Logs stay content-free.** The overlay logs only state changes as enums.
    - `OverlayView` holds content (transcripts, previews), so it is never logged and never written to E2E results. The E2E records the state enum and the button set only.

## v2 rulings after four adversarial reviews (UI/UX, platform, security/privacy, product/architecture). These override the text above.

**Waits and re-planning (all four reviews)**
1. **Preview and pending paste never wait inside `OutputExecutor._gate`.**
   - The sequence: plan, release, then wait (preview or focus). Then take the gate again, take a fresh `targets.Snapshot()` and re-plan before the swap.
   - Exit, EndingSession and a cancel tap cancel any pending wait, through an executor-owned CTS, before `FlushAsync`.
2. **Pending paste re-plans with the newly focused element as `atStart`**, so every safety rule runs again: password, elevated, terminal and remote Reply.
   - Only a focus change that follows a user mouse-up seen by the hook counts. Injected input, dialogs and focus stolen by an app don't.
   - The new focus must be in the same process as the original target.
   - It's detected by a UIA `FocusChangedEventHandler` on its own MTA thread, never on the hook thread, then waits for mouse-up before pasting.
   - The timeout is 10 s. A timeout or a hold-key tap copies, as today's CopiedNotEditable does.

**Preview (UI/UX #1 and #13, platform #2, security #4 and #6)**

3. **Capture is armed on the hook thread first, and the card shows only after the hook confirms.**
   - Each captured key is swallowed until its own key-up, auto-repeat included, whatever the capture state does in between.
   - Injected keys are never swallowed and never trigger anything. The existing marker check runs before capture.
   - Modifier state is tracked inside the reducer.
   - VKs are never logged.
   - A hook-thread deadline of 20 s releases capture even if the UI thread hangs.
4. **Keys:** Enter inserts, Esc discards, C copies, and PgUp/PgDn/Up/Down scroll.
   - The card also has Insert, Copy and Discard buttons.
   - Buttons use `AllowFocusOnInteraction=false` and `IsTabStop=false`.
5. **Capture ends on anything else: another key, a mouse click outside the card, a foreground change, or the 20 s timeout.**
   - Ending this way means "keep in history" (the row already exists). It is never an unrequested copy and never an Insert. The notice is "Preview kept in history".
   - A hold-key tap during preview does the same.
   - A new hold during preview is Busy, as today.
6. **Insert re-plans against a fresh snapshot.** A target that changed (a tab switch to a login page) is refused or copied by the normal rules.
7. **Trigger: Shift held at release.**
   - Only if Shift has been down for 250 ms or more and wasn't part of a mode-cycle tap in the last 250 ms.
   - Plus `PreviewModes`, a list of modes that always preview (default empty).
   - `PreviewLongOverWords` is deferred.

**Undo (all four reviews: Ctrl+Z is unsafe)**

8. **There is no Ctrl+Z.**
   - "Use raw" copies the raw transcript and says "Raw transcript copied: press Ctrl+V".
   - It's offered for 5 s after a rewritten paste, where `RunResult.Rewritten` is new and true only when the LLM's output was used.
   - It shows as a small separate pill window at the notch's right edge, hit-testable only over its button. The Done card itself stays click-through.
   - The offer is withdrawn on any key the hook sees and on a new hold.

**Notices and precedence (UI/UX #6 to #9, product #9)**

9. **Run notices show on the overlay's Error card.**
   - While `SHQueryUserNotificationState` reports a full-screen app (D3D or presentation mode), yolime falls back to the sound plus a tray balloon, and the tray icon shows an error dot until the menu is opened.
   - Started, AlreadyRunning and startup problems stay as balloons.
   - `Notices.Text(n, onCard)`: on the card, the copy says "Retry" (button). In a balloon it says "Retry from the tray".
   - MicDenied's card has an "Open settings" button.
10. **Precedence:**
    - A new hold replaces Done, Error and Use raw.
    - Preview takes priority over everything; a hold during it is Busy, and the Busy text shows inline on the Preview card.
    - CapWarning shows as Listening's countdown.
    - Each run shows one notice: the model takes the run's notice and ignores the executor's Copied when the run has its own (as QuietCopy already does). The executor's notices go through the same `NoticeLimiter`.
11. **Error card timing:**
    - It shows for 6 s plus 40 ms per character, capped at 15 s, and pauses while the mouse is over it.
    - Overlay Retry retries the run on the card, by its history id: audio retry or transcript retry. The result goes to the clipboard, because there is no start target, and Done says "Copied: press Ctrl+V".
    - A hotkey tap while a retryable Error card is up also retries it (spec: "a tap of the hotkey"). This needs a new `IdleTap` effect from the hold machine, which emits it only for a sub-300 ms tap with nothing else pressed.
12. **Done:**
    - "-> {display name} · N words · 1.9 s", where the time is `RunTiming.OutputDoneMs`, the latency the user feels.
    - It shows for 1.2 s, or 4 s when Copied (the spec's 4 s).
    - The display name is the process's `FileDescription`, cached, falling back to the process name.

**States, from the spec (UI/UX #11, product #8)**

13. **Listening:**
    - the mode icon (a Segoe Fluent glyph per mode) and label, the app's name, the elapsed time, and the level meter;
    - for a latched recording, "Tap to stop";
    - in the last 10 s before the cap, a countdown.
14. **Timing:** the overlay appears when the hold reaches 300 ms (`RecordingChanged(true)` fires there already). A short tap never shows it.
15. **Context line: the snippet is run through `ContextFilter` before it's shown.** A rejected clipboard shows "Copied text skipped (looks like a key)". It shows at most 60 characters.
16. **Working:** the streamed preview is throttled to 4 Hz and shows the last line only. Level updates are coalesced to 30 Hz.

**Rendering and platform (platform #5 to #10, product #11)**

17. **The island is created once, at startup, hidden.**
    - WS_EX_LAYERED stays on permanently. Only WS_EX_TRANSPARENT toggles, as the prototype does (the plan text is corrected).
    - Rounded corners use `SetWindowRgn(CreateRoundRectRgn)`.
    - The HWND is sized from the root's measured `DesiredSize` at the target monitor's DPI on each show. A width cap of 560 DIP makes long text wrap.
    - A long-lived `UISettings` handles `ColorValuesChanged`/`TextScaleFactorChanged` (marshalled), and `RequestedTheme` follows `ActualTheme`.
    - High contrast uses `SystemColor*` brushes and an explicit 1 px border.
18. **`HideOverlayFromCapture` defaults to on.**
    - `SetWindowDisplayAffinity` is checked and logged as an enum, and re-applied after style changes.
    - The pixel-check E2E turns it off. A separate scenario checks a BitBlt doesn't show the overlay when it's on.
19. **Sounds are generated in code at startup** (no build script). PCM goes into `Marshal.AllocHGlobal` for the process's lifetime and plays with `PlaySound(SND_MEMORY|SND_ASYNC|SND_NODEFAULT)`, never from the hook callback.
20. **Narrator:**
    - A dedicated hidden TextBlock carries `AutomationProperties.LiveSetting`, and its peer raises notifications. The root Border has no peer.
    - `ImportantAll` for errors, `MostRecent` for state.
    - Nothing is announced while the mic records (Listening and mode cycles), because it would be transcribed.
    - Announced: Done, Error and Preview shown, the last one with its text, since the user asked for it.
    - Narrator is verified manually (task 11). The Sandbox can't prove it.
21. **NearCaret is deferred.** TopCentre and BottomCentre only.
22. **Logging:** `OverlayView` overrides `PrintMembers` to leave out its text fields, and the no-content test covers it.

**Plumbing (product #6 and #10)**

23. **The coordinator passes `RunRequest.Progress`**, and retries too. The adapter enqueues each event straight onto the DispatcherQueue in order (not `Progress<T>`).
24. **New task 7b, App glue:**
    - the coordinator -> `OverlayModel` -> view adapter, the Tick `DispatcherQueueTimer`, and the `INoticeSink` routing (overlay or balloon);
    - the Retry, Use raw and Open settings handlers.

    It comes before the views (task 8).

**E2E additions:**
- Keyboard focus stays in the form's box after a click on an overlay button, checked with `GetGUIThreadInfo`, not just the foreground window.
- A click during Listening falls through to the window underneath.
- Preview: Insert, Discard and Copy, and a stray key ending the preview keeps the box and clipboard unchanged.
- Use raw copies.
- Pending paste into Box2 works. Pending paste to a password box is refused.
- Corners are transparent (pixel).
- Hide from capture.

**Task order:** 0, 1 (model), 2 (coordinator plumbing + IdleTap), 3 (settings), 4 (preview, Core), 5 (hook capture), 6 (Use raw), 7 (pending paste, Core), 7b (glue), 8 (views), 9 (sounds, focus waiter, capture wiring), 10 (E2E), 11 (manual).
